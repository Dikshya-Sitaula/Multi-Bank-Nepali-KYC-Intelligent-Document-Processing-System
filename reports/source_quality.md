## Thresholds (from `config/source_forms.yaml`)

- Minimum native resolution of scanned content: 120.0 DPI (vector pages are resolution-independent and always pass)
- Minimum ink contrast (median grey minus 0.1th percentile grey): 80.0

A form is **clean** (training template) only if every page passes both thresholds.

Why these criteria: a scanned page is one embedded bitmap, and rasterizing it at 300 DPI cannot add detail, so its native DPI is the honest resolution. Global Laplacian variance and RMS contrast are kept as information only: JPEG block artefacts inflate the Laplacian, and a crisp vector form printed in light orange ink has low RMS contrast.
